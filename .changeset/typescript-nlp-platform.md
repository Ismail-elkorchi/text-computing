Reframe the platform around Text Computing, Capability Packs, and Textpack
Forge. Replace npm-package-owned resource bindings with semantic slot, role,
schema, and resource bindings, and remove the redundant manifest engine map.

Capability Packs can now describe deployable artifacts independently of the
toolchain that produced them or the internal workspace module that executes
them.
